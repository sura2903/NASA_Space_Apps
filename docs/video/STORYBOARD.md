# 30-second video storyboard: "Ten seasons vs twenty-four"

The same storyboard as the slide deck, in the repo so it can be opened without the deck. Nimalan owns the video;
these are suggestions. Graphics and data are in this folder (see README.md).

**Deadline:** the official Space Apps submission closes **Sunday 15 Nov 2026, 11:59 PM local time**. Please post
the finished video (unlisted link) in issue #20 by **8:00 PM Pacific on 15 Nov**. That leaves time to add it to the
project page and save the final submission.

**The idea: a year is a square.** One motif at four scales:
- **Memory:** 10 squares, the seasons a farmer remembers.
- **Record:** 24 squares, NASA's record, red where crops failed.
- **State:** the squares tile into the 185 map cells, one for each place in Tamil Nadu.
- **Farmer:** they come back as the 24-square plan cards on a phone, e.g. "16 of 24".

**Look and sound:**
- App colours: deep green, paper and monsoon orange.
- Rain that cuts to silence at 2016, and a soft tick per square.
- Captions burned in (judges often watch muted), in English with a Tamil option.
