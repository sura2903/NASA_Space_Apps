    Words used here

    Honest limits

    • NASA POWER temperature and GPM IMERG rain are regional grids. They describe the area around your village, not your exact field. Satellite rain also smooths the very biggest downpours.
    • Crop water needs and traits are approximate averages, and the scores are simple rules we can explain, not a trained model.
    • Climate what-ifs are rough scalings to explore, not forecasts.
    • Soil nutrients, pests, markets and water allocations need local knowledge. The printed sheet lists what to check.
